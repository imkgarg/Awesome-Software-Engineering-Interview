# Mock Interview Platforms

- [Practick](https://practick.io) - Free peer-to-peer mock interviews for DSA, system design (HLD) and low-level design. You interview someone to earn an interview, and every question comes with an interviewer guide and scoring rubric.
- [Pramp](https://www.pramp.com) - Free peer-to-peer mock interviews, now hosted on Aced Practice.
- [interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io) - Anonymous mock interviews with senior engineers from top companies (paid).
